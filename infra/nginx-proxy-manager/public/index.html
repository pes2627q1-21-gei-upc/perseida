Perseida

Perseida is a mobile app to learn about and follow astronomical events: eclipses, meteor showers, Moon phases and launches. You can follow events, organise observation outings, talk with the community and learn with a daily quiz.

It is an academic project of the Software Engineering Project course (PES) at the Barcelona School of Informatics (FIB, UPC), made by the Perseida team. You sign in with your Google account.
